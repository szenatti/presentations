Fabric Finance Planner – Synthetic Dataset

Purpose:
Synthetic finance actuals for a Microsoft Fabric + Rayfin budget-entry demonstration.

Period:
1 January 2024 to 30 June 2026

Contents:
- 6,000 finance transactions
- 8 departments
- 20 expense categories
- 35 vendors
- 5 Australian regions
- 8 projects
- Monthly date dimension
- Rayfin BudgetEntry schema reference

Built-in demonstration patterns:
- 2026 Information Technology / Software & SaaS and Cloud Services have elevated expenditure.
- 2026 Operations / Contractors has elevated expenditure.
- 2026 Marketing / Advertising is relatively lower.
- 2026 Human Resources / Training is relatively lower.
- Travel, campaigns, utilities and hardware contain seasonal patterns.

Recommended demo filters:
- June 2026 / Information Technology / Software & SaaS
- May 2026 / Information Technology / Cloud Services
- April 2026 / Operations / Contractors
- June 2026 / Marketing / Advertising
- March 2026 / Human Resources / Training

Rayfin budget key:
financialYear + monthNumber + departmentKey + categoryKey
